# The library catalog is split into independent modules for search, loans and notices

* Status: accepted, partially superseded by ADR-0010
* Date: 2026-05-09
* Deciders: Project owner (scope `product`)

Record invented for the KBOL tests: the Faro project does not exist.

## Context and Problem Statement

The Faro catalog is a single module that searches books, manages loans and sends return notices. A change in the notices forces a deployment of the search, and a failure of the mail server leaves the whole library without loans.

## Decision Drivers

* Deploy each part without touching the others.
* A failure of the notices must not prevent lending.
* Keep a single data contract for the books.

## Considered Options

### Option A — Three modules with a common contract

Search, loans and notices are separated and share the book schema.

### Option B — One module with internal queues

The catalog stays as one and the notices move to a queue.

### Option C — One service per reading room

Each reading room has its own catalog.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: "A", because it is the only one that separates failures without duplicating the book schema.

### Positive Consequences

* The notices are deployed without stopping the loans.

### Negative Consequences

* There are three deployments to coordinate.

## Validation

Contract tests between the three modules with a sample catalog of twenty books.

## More Information

ADR-0010 later supersedes the notices part: they move to an external service. The split between search and loans remains in force.
